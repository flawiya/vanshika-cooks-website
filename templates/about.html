Hi, I'm Vanshika! My culinary love story started years ago in my mother's kitchen, mesmerized by the crackle of mustard seeds in hot ghee and the intoxicating aroma of slow-simmering weekend curries.

As I grew older and watched friends struggle with cooking, I noticed a common frustration: most traditional recipes rely on vague terms like "add salt as per taste" or "cook until done," leaving beginner cooks guessing and anxious.

That is why I created @vanshikacooks: a warm, modern culinary sanctuary where recipes are broken down with mathematical precision (grams, cups, exact minutes), culinary secrets are demystified, and every single dish is available in both English and Hindi so that delicious food brings generations together.
