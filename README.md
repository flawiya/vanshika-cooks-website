# Vanshika Swadisht Faral (वंशिका स्वादिष्ट फराळ 🪔)
**Official Diwali Faral & Festive Sweets E-Commerce Store for [@vanshikacooks](https://www.instagram.com/vanshikacooks/)**

An artisanal, modern festive food and e-commerce selling website offering authentic homemade traditional Maharashtrian & Indian Diwali Faral, pure desi ghee sweets, and luxury festive gift hampers with a seamless **English / मराठी-हिन्दी** bilingual toggle, **Interactive Faral Box Checkout**, and direct **Dual WhatsApp Notification** to owners (`+91 8623865447` / `+91 8999585283`).

---

## 🌟 Key Features

1. **Dedicated Diwali Faral & Sweets Selling Platform:**
   - Handcrafted traditional festive foods: *Bhajani Chakli, Pure Desi Ghee Besan Ladoo, Fresh Coconut Karanji, Poha Chivda, Shankarpali, Tikha Lasun Sev, Motichoor Ladoo, and Authentic Anarse*.
   - Pack selection with clear weight-based pricing: **250g, 500g, and 1kg**.
   - Currency switcher supporting Indian Rupees (₹ INR) and British Pounds (£ GBP).

2. **Delivery Location & Flat ₹60 Shipping Rule:**
   - **Local Direct Delivery:** Exclusively across **Chhatrapati Sambhaji Nagar (Aurangabad), Maharashtra** with a flat **₹60.00** delivery charge.
   - **Outstation Delivery:** For other locations across Maharashtra (Pune, Mumbai, Nashik, Jalna, etc.) and India, customers can submit an outstation request or message on WhatsApp for custom parcel / courier shipping quotes.

3. **Cash on Delivery with 50% Initial Advance:**
   - Because all Faral is freshly handcrafted in small kitchen batches in 100% pure cow ghee upon order, a **50% advance booking payment** is required via UPI to confirm the batch preparation slot.
   - The remaining **50% balance is payable in Cash on Delivery (COD)** upon doorstep arrival in Chhatrapati Sambhaji Nagar.

4. **Unique Order ID Generation:**
   - Every order generates an unique, human-readable ID (e.g., `#VSF-2026-XXXX` for local orders, `#VSF-OUT-XXXX` for outstation inquiries).

5. **Instant WhatsApp Order Dispatch to Kitchen Owners:**
   - Direct integration routing complete order receipts to official kitchen owner numbers:
     - **Owner 1:** `+91 8623865447`
     - **Owner 2:** `+91 8999585283`
   - Messages are formatted automatically with:
     - 🆔 Unique Order ID
     - 👤 Customer Name
     - 📞 WhatsApp / Contact Phone Number
     - 📍 Complete Delivery Address & Landmark
     - 📅 Requested Delivery Date (Order Needed Date)
     - 📦 Ordered Items, Pack Weights & Quantities
     - 💰 Financial breakdown (Faral Subtotal, ₹60 Delivery Fee, Total Amount)
     - 💳 50% Advance Payable Now & 50% COD Balance

6. **Interactive Faral Box & Checkout System:**
   - Real-time cart calculation (`/checkout` & `/order`) with quantity adjusters (`+` / `-`), item removal, and quick-add controls for all Faral items and festive hampers.
   - Printable order receipt with celebratory diya animations.

7. **Full English / मराठी-हिन्दी Bilingual Toggle:**
   - Complete bilingual toggle across the entire site including product descriptions, pack sizes, ingredients, taste profiles, delivery terms, and order summaries.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **Backend:** Python / Flask
- **Database:** SQLite3 (`vanshika_cooks.db`) with automatic self-initialization (`init_db.py`)
- **Frontend:** Jinja2 Templates, Modern Responsive CSS3 (Warm Saffron, Deep Maroon & Gold Diwali Festive Palette)
- **Deployment:** Render (Gunicorn WSGI)
